The takeaway

A snapshot, not a universal rule.

The analysis of the 15 selected student responses shows that daily screen time is spread across four reported categories. The 3–4-hour, 4–5-hour and more-than-5-hour categories are tied as the most frequent, with four students each. The median category is 4–5 hours. The estimated mean is 4.10 hours per day, but this is only an approximation because the survey recorded ranges rather than exact hours and the “More than 5 hours” group has no upper limit. The project demonstrates how frequency tables and graphs help organise and interpret survey data, while showing why assumptions and limitations should be stated clearly.
